Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

The Lam7a app connects only to Lam7a’s own Instagram and TikTok accounts. It does not collect data about our followers or any other users.

What the app stores

  • The account ID of Lam7a’s own Instagram and TikTok accounts.
  • The access token those platforms issue to Lam7a, kept privately and used only to publish Lam7a’s posts.
  • The IDs and times of posts the app published, so we know what went out.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent or share any data.
  • We do not read, collect or store followers’ personal information, messages or contacts.
  • We do not use the data for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Instagram or TikTok account settings (apps and websites / connected apps). When access is removed, the stored token stops working and we delete it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a direct message to @Lam7a.News on Instagram or TikTok.
